What is changing

Companies are shifting to small teams amplified by AI

AI now does real work at every stage of a process, inside the right controls. Organisations that rebuild around AI move faster and carry less cost. Organisations that add AI to old workflows mostly stay where they were.

TodayMany layers. Slow and expensive.

NextSmall, exceptional teams, amplified by AI.

  • Generalists
  • Specialists
  • AI agents

Small, exceptional teams × AI leverage > large teams × manual process.

The gap

Almost every enterprise has AI in the plan. Very few have it in their results. The technology is ready. What is missing is the work of building it into the systems, decisions and habits of each organisation

What it takes

  1. Go deep into your context

    Workflows designed around how your teams really work, exceptions included.

    Not choosing a tool first, then bending the work around it

  2. Size the value before the build

    Every opportunity measured in your own numbers and tied to a business outcome.

    Not solving problems where the value never shows up

  3. Treat adoption as the job

    Change management that links daily use to incentives and to results.

    Not calling go-live the finish line

How we engage

Five stages. You know what you get at each one.

We agree scope and success measures first, and tie them to a business result. Management decides at the end of each stage. No stage starts until you sign off the stage before.

  2. Stage 1

    Discover

    Understand the decision, the data and the constraints.

    • Map systems, data and workflows
    • Baseline the KPIs on your own numbers
    • Agree scope and success measures

    You getA decision brief

  3. Stage 2

    Design

    Shape the product or solution, and its controls.

    • Read the systems and logs, not only the workshops
    • Co-design the target workflow with your teams
    • Design the controls in from the start

    You getA solution and control design

  4. Stage 3

    Build

    Develop, integrate and test with your team.

    • Build inside your live systems
    • Backtest on history before go-live
    • Release every week, shaped by the users

    You getA working, integrated product

  5. Stage 4

    Launch

    Go live, with security and compliance signed off.

    • Shadow mode first, then a staged rollout
    • Security and compliance sign-off
    • Train the teams that will run it

    You getA signed-off go-live

  6. Stage 5

    Evolve

    Monitor, improve and extend what is in production.

    • Track adoption and value, with an owner per metric
    • Extend to more products, teams and entities
    • Hand over code and runbooks to your team

    You getA monitored, improving service
